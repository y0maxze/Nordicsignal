# NewsWeb revalidation and truthful freshness

The first archive adapter queried historical days once. This could miss source-provided corrections published after a day was closed. Each background batch still performs at most one bounded list request. Today's hourly query takes priority, followed by expired leases, finalising yesterday, and daily revisits of the last seven days. Initial unseen days follow newest first. Failed/truncated older days become retry-eligible after one day, and other older days after 30 days, once initial backfill permits. Older retries are ordered by oldest attempt. Active leases are skipped. No coverage completeness is inferred.

A successful response records its actual completion time, including truncated responses whose limitation remains explicit. Failed attempts retain the last successful completion timestamp and existing documents. Legacy payloads without that timestamp remain unknown rather than reconstructing a success date from an attempt. The Stock UI distinguishes last attempt from last successful market archive fetch, and explains that neither proves full issuer coverage. Existing exact issuer and document matching remains unchanged.

Regression tests cover later explicit source corrections, failed revisits with retained evidence, old-window revisits, priority of unseen history, active leases and UI disclosure. PWA shell v22. Signal, score, Opportunity, High Conviction, sizing, push and secrets are unchanged. Authenticated production UI/PWA/push inspection remains blocked by the previously observed Cloudflare robot verification; deploy checks do not certify that experience.
